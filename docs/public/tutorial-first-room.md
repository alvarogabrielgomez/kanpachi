# Your first room

By the end of one of these you will have a room open, a friend inside it, and a
game reachable between the two machines with nothing else exposed. It takes
about ten minutes.

**What you need:** two machines, one on each side, and a way to send a line of
text between them. No account, no router configuration, no port forwarding,
nothing to pay.

## Pick the machine you are hosting from

Both cover the same ground and reach the same room. They differ at every step,
because one is a window and the other is a terminal.

- **[On Windows](tutorial-first-room-windows.md)**: the installer, the window,
  and buttons.
- **[On Linux](tutorial-first-room-linux.md)**: the `.deb`, and one command per
  step.

Your friend can be on either. They paste the code you send and Kanpachi does the
rest, so pick by the machine that opens the room.

## What both of them cover

1. Installing Kanpachi.
2. Choosing the name the room shows you by.
3. Choosing the registry your rooms open on. Kanpachi ships without one.
4. Opening the room, which takes about a minute.
5. Handing out the invite code.
6. Your friend joining with it.
7. Choosing the game, which is what opens any port at all.
8. Closing the room.
